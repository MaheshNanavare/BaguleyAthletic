Who we are

Our story.
Our vision.

Baguley Athletic Football Club — affectionately known as The Badgers — is a progressive, community-driven club proud to represent our local area, built on hard work, professional governance and a deep connection to the community we serve.

A new chapter

Every great club
has a turning point.

We aren't just building a football team; we are building a sustainable sporting institution. Driven by an ambitious three-person leadership team alongside Club Chairman and former England international David Platt, Baguley Athletic is transitioning into a modern, forward-thinking club with a clear elite-level focus.

Following a comprehensive restructuring of our leadership and football operations, we have modernised the entire infrastructure — from financial oversight and commercial strategy to match-day operations — and laid down professional foundations designed to withstand competitive non-league football.

We are a club that respects its roots but refuses to stand still. Every training session, every administrative decision and every match-day is executed with a higher standard in mind.

The road to Step Six

Building a club
that can compete.

Our ambition is clear and unapologetic: we are building a club capable of competing at a semi-professional level. We judge our success not just by the trophies in the cabinet, but by the sustainable pathways we create for players, coaches and our community.

The strategic road-map rests on three pillars.

Elite development

Recruit on
merit.

Performance-based recruitment and objective player assessment. Rigorous scouting and grading make sure every player who wears the badge has what it takes to win promotions.

A complete pathway

Leave
no one behind.

The First Team climbing the pyramid. The Development Squad nurturing elite under-21 talent. A competitive, fully integrated Women and Girls setup.

Community wealth building

Grow the
whole area.

Through our community membership scheme, The Sett, we bridge grassroots football and local independent businesses — so as the club succeeds, our community succeeds with us.
