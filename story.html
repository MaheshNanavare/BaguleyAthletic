Who we are

Our story.
Our vision.

Baguley Athletic Football Club—affectionately known as The Badgers—is a progressive, community-driven football club proud to represent our local area. We are built on a foundation of hard work, professional governance, and a deep connection to the community we serve.

We aren't just building a football team; we are building a sustainable sporting institution. Driven by an ambitious three-person leadership team alongside Club Chairman and former England international David Platt, Baguley Athletic is actively transitioning into a modern, forward-thinking club with a clear elite-level focus.

A new chapter

Every great club
has a turning point.

For Baguley Athletic, that chapter is being written right now. Following a comprehensive restructuring of our leadership and football operations, the club has entered an exciting new era. We have modernised our entire infrastructure—from our financial oversight and commercial strategies to our match-day operations.

By bringing together a dedicated committee with decades of combined experience in football administration and strategic development, we have laid down professional foundations designed to withstand the pressures of competitive, high-level non-league football.

We are a club that respects its roots but refuses to stand still. Every training session, every administrative decision, and every match-day is executed with a higher standard in mind.

The road to Step Six

Building a club that
can compete.

Our ambition is clear and unapologetic: we are building a club capable of competing at a semi-professional level. We judge our success not just by the trophies in the cabinet, but by the sustainable pathways we create for players, coaches, and our community.

Our strategic road-map rests on three core pillars:

01

Elite
development.

We believe in performance-based recruitment and objective player assessment. Rigorous scouting and grading ensure every player who wears the badge has what it takes to win promotions.

02

A complete
pathway.

The First Team climbing the pyramid. The Development Squad nurturing elite under-21 talent. A competitive, fully integrated Women & Girls setup. A true community club leaves no one behind.

03

Community
wealth building.

Through our community membership scheme, The Sett, we bridge grassroots football and local independent businesses — so as the club succeeds, our community succeeds with us.
