Chairman's welcome

“We are striving for progression and success.”

I'm delighted to become Chairman of Baguley Athletic FC, a club I've come to know well over the last two seasons. The ambition of our volunteers is highly motivating, and while my background is there for the players and management to draw upon, it's our off-field vision that will truly help us prosper.

We'll achieve that by building an inclusive club, establishing a unified identity, and deeply supporting our local community.

David Platt · Club Chairman
