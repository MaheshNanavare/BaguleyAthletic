Identity and
presence.

Since joining we have gained real momentum, on the pitch and off it. In large part that has come from focusing on two core principles — identity and presence — and the two go hand in hand.

Identity means smart new kits and new training and travel apparel, so that players, volunteers and prospective commercial partners can feel proud of what we stand for and how we look. Presence means being involved, deeply, in the local community.

We have gifted front of shirt sponsorship to two charities. The home kit carries the logo of BW3, funded by businesses to invest in local schoolchildren. The away shirt carries Woodhouse Park Family Centre, a not-for-profit free childcare programme for families that need it.

David Platt · Club Chairman

Pitch & stadium advertising

Seen all week.

On matchdays

Your pitch-side board reaches a wide, diverse demographic of thousands of visitors. Because our ground proudly hosts Baguley Athletic, Wythenshawe Town and Wythenshawe Town Laces (formerly Manchester Laces), your brand gets triple the exposure — welcoming football communities from across the region every weekend.

Throughout the week

The visibility doesn't stop when the final whistle blows. Our external stadium boards face directly onto Altrincham Road (A556), a major local traffic artery. With an estimated 60,000 cars passing the ground every week, your business gains constant, repetitive exposure to daily commuters.
