We are proud to officially announce the renewal of our ground-sharing agreement with Wythenshawe Town Football Club for the upcoming 2026/2027 season.

Collaboration is at the heart of community sport, and extending this partnership ensures our players, staff, and supporters continue to benefit from excellent facilities. This stability is crucial as we look to build on our recent progress both on and off the pitch.

We would like to express our sincere gratitude to the committee and staff at Wythenshawe Town for their continued hospitality and shared vision for local football.

Here’s to another successful year of collaboration and sporting achievement.
