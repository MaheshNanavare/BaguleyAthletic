The Baguley Athletic family is growing!

We are absolutely thrilled to officially welcome our brand-new Baguley Athletic Women’s First Team to the club!

Making the switch from Ashton on Mersey, this incredible group of players joins us at a massive moment for Baguley Athletic as we continue to build, grow, and push for the future. We couldn't be prouder to have them wearing the badge and representing the club on the pitch this season.

Big things are happening, and our commitment to championing and expanding women’s football in the community has never been stronger.

Welcome to the club, ladies!
