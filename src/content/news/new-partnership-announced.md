At Baguley Athletic FC, our commitment to the community goes far beyond the pitch. We believe in using our platform to lift up the local organisations making a tangible difference every day.

We are incredibly proud to officially announce that we have gifted our away kit front-of-shirt sponsorship rights to the Woodhouse Park Family Centre.

This initiative builds on our recent decision to gift our home shirt sponsorship to BW3. While that partnership targets strategic local improvement, our collaboration with Woodhouse Park Family Centre focuses directly on day-to-day community support—ensuring local families know exactly where to turn for help, how to access resources, and how to get involved.

> "We're really grateful to Baguley Athletic for choosing to support us in this way. Having the Centre’s name on the front of the away shirt helps raise awareness of the services we provide and the families we support. It’s a brilliant example of a local club stepping up for its community."
>
> — Clare North, Manager of Woodhouse Park Family Centre

Baguley Athletic Chair, David Platt added "Baguley Athletic plays on the pitch, but we are just as committed to what we can do off it. By gifting our away shirt sponsorship to Woodhouse Park Family Centre, we can use our visibility to shine a light on an organisation that supports local families every single day. We are delighted to raise our own identity and presence in the community by helping the centre, and will continue our support of it through the season."

A huge thank you to everyone involved in making this partnership happen during our recent launch at Ericstan Park. Together, we are building a stronger community.

Learn more about the Centre's vital work at [whpfamilycentre.co.uk](https://whpfamilycentre.co.uk).
