An incredible evening of teamwork and community spirit at the Runway Visitor Park for the BW3 (Business Working with Wythenshawe) Charity Fire Engine Pull!

Baguley Athletic Football Club was proud to line up alongside local businesses and organisations to support BW3’s vital work in creating career opportunities and raising aspirations for young people across the region.

The task required absolute synchronisation and maximum effort: pulling a heavy airport fire engine (better yet, the heavier replacement) over a 20-metre course.

We are absolutely thrilled to share that the Baguley Athletic team put in a phenomenal performance and walked away as this year's champions!

Events like this showcase the true strength of the Wythenshawe community. A massive thank you to the organisers at BW3, the volunteers, and everyone who donated to such an impactful cause.

We’re incredibly proud of the lads for bringing the trophy home!
